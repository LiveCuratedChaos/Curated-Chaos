## Reading and maintenance rules

- IDs below are stable. Split implementation stories under an ID, for example `CC-FIN-02.a`, without deleting the parent requirement or narrowing its acceptance.
- **D — development slice accepted:** implemented with recorded bounded development/browser/hosted evidence. **P — partial:** reusable model, screen, adapter or fixture exists, but the requirement is incomplete. **N — not evidenced:** no complete implementation identified in the reviewed source. These statuses do not certify production readiness.
- **Production:** **Open** means the release gate remains unsatisfied; **Gate** means explicit release, migration or external approval is required. No row in this baseline is certified production-complete.
- **First** means included in the first-private-release target or its release foundations. **Retained** means the blueprint explicitly permits staged expansion, without cancellation. **Cross-cutting** applies wherever the associated feature ships. The lead must settle ambiguous sequencing explicitly; ambiguity must not silently remove a commitment.
- Every acceptance test must cover permitted behavior and denied behavior. Fixtures alone cannot certify a real provider; a displayed card alone cannot certify an engine; client filtering alone cannot certify authorization.
- Reuse named evidence. Extend proven domain models and boundaries before proposing replacement technology. A later implementation must demonstrate why reuse is insufficient before rebuilding an accepted capability.
- The historical completion plan remains an append-only evidence log. This ledger is the current coverage map; an integration backlog should reference its IDs and link actual implementation/test/acceptance evidence as work closes.

## Reconciliations that must not be hidden by development status

### Routine autosave/Undo versus reviewed mutation

The blueprint requires autosave of routine edits with Undo/history while preserving mandatory approval for sensitive actions and email-derived task/event/application changes. The current private preview's explicit reviews and one-use mutation controls are valuable, accepted safety slices; they do **not** implement the complete routine-edit autosave promise.

The shared mutation contract should classify operations, not force all operations into one UX. Reuse optimistic versions, exact acknowledgement/reread, idempotency and audit. Add durable routine-edit autosave plus reversible history under CC-HIST-01; retain explicit proposal/approval/strong-reauth gates wherever CC-AUTO-01, CC-ID-03 or provider rules require them. Acceptance must prove that autosave never silently upgrades a sensitive proposal into an executed action and that uncertain writes are not blindly replayed.

### Temporary drafts versus durable offline editing

Current preview drafts can survive selected validation/network failures in page memory, but are deliberately cleared when the tab hides, the session ends or some temporary views expire. This is an explicit privacy boundary of the development preview, **not** completion of CC-OFF-01. A PWA manifest and cross-tab channel do not add durable storage, offline operations, encryption or conflict handling.

F1/F2/F6/F7 must define what may be retained per device, how it is encrypted/unlocked, how operation origin/version/idempotency are recorded, and how sign-out/revocation interacts with queued work. The UI must distinguish a temporary draft from saved-local, sync-pending, conflict and synced states. Acceptance must include app/tab restart, reconnect, other-device edits, revoked access and storage eviction. Do not “solve” privacy by silently dropping the agreed offline workflow or “solve” draft retention by caching all private records without a device-security design.

### Snapshots, source refresh and cross-device synchronization

Saved Gmail/Chase/calendar copies, app-record synchronization and provider refresh are three different capabilities. Existing adapters/import/recovery/freshness components should be integrated, not rebuilt, but none makes snapshot data continuously current. The Mac helper cannot refresh while its Mac is unavailable. Any broader provider/path, credentials, cost or data-processing scope remains a real feasibility/access decision. The Brief must continue to label those limits accurately while F4/F7 are completed.

### Family and document placeholders versus engines

An `owner`/`adult` membership table is not a child/guardian model. A project named “Budget” is not zero-based budgeting. A project tagged “Grocery” is not a concurrent quantity/recurrence/sharing engine. An encrypted recovery archive is not a general Export Center or document library. Preserve those useful primitives while measuring completion against the acceptance columns above.
